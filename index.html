About

Experience

Battle-tested full-stack PM who's navigated everything from early-stage chaos to enterprise transformations, with the technical chops to speak engineering and the strategic mindset to drive business outcomes. The journey started with a journalism degree that somehow led to web development at Snowbound Software, building marketing funnels and discovering a love for solving user problems through code. Spent years honing UX development skills at OHO Interactive and PARTNERSsimons, then launched DSCO to help digital agencies scale their technical capabilities.

That consulting work caught Ogilvy's attention, where I evolved from developer to Director of Digital Technology, finally understanding that the most interesting problems lived at the intersection of business strategy and technical execution. The transition to Product Management felt inevitable—starting at Copyright Clearance Center where I scaled AI-powered search platforms from pilot programs to Fortune 50 adoption, then diving deeper into enterprise strategy at Elastic Path's eCommerce platform.

Recent years have been about tackling increasingly complex challenges: consolidating a $5M healthcare ecosystem at Fueled, managing strategic partnerships with Royal Bank of Canada at CircleBlack, and building Pando's AI-powered investment platform from 4-person startup to 30+ employees serving multiple asset classes. Now based in Raleigh, exploring how AI can make product management more intuitive while enjoying the occasional escape from startup intensity.
